Verint Customer Success 6 minutes, for CSMs

CSM micro learning · Opening a value-focused EBR

Lead With the Outcome

You have an EBR with Meridian Mutual next week. The first five minutes decide whether it becomes a status update or a value conversation.

  • Open the EBR with the customer’s own goal, not a product update
  • Show progress in the customer’s metrics
  • Name a data gap without losing the room

Before

Find the outcome

Go back to the success plan. What did Meridian Mutual say they wanted to achieve, and how would they measure it? Look for the customer’s words, not ours: a business result their leadership already tracks.

Hold on to this. If you can’t state the outcome in one sentence, that’s your first job, before you build a single slide.

During

The first two minutes

Activity first

“Let’s start with adoption by team. Usage is up across the board since last quarter.”

Accurate, but it’s our story. The customer has to connect it to their goals on their own.

Outcome first

“At kickoff, you told us the priority was bringing handle time down before open enrollment. Here’s where it stands against the baseline, and here’s what drove it.”

Same data. Adoption becomes the reason, not the headline.

Ask: Could the customer’s executive repeat your opening to their own boss?

During

Name the gap

When you can’t measure something yet, say so: “We can’t see after-call work yet. Here’s how we’ll close that by next quarter.” Customers trust a CSM who names a gap. They stop trusting one who hides it behind usage stats.

Hold on to this. A named gap with a plan builds more credibility than a full slide of numbers that miss the point.

After

Lock it in

  1. Update the success plan. Capture anything that changed, so the next review starts from here.
  2. Send a recap within 24 hours. Outcomes, progress, and next goals, in the customer’s words.
  3. Share one thing that landed with your manager. It becomes the starting point for your next coaching conversation.

Three quick calls

Each one is a moment you will face. Pick the best move. You need 2 of 3 to continue.

Two days before the EBR, Meridian Mutual’s sponsor emails: “Things are going well. We don’t really need a formal review.”

What’s your best move?

Meridian Mutual’s top outcome is reducing after-call work, but that data isn’t available yet.
